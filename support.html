Common Issues

Flash Panel not appearing?

Ensure the extension is enabled and refresh the page. Some restricted pages (for example Chrome Web Store pages) block script injection by design.

Selection bubble not showing?

Please make sure "Show Flash Panel" is turned on in Settings, then reload the current tab once.

Notion Sync failed?

Re-authorize your Notion account in extension settings, and ensure your target database has been shared with "AI Memo Wrap".
