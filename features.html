Product capabilities

Features built for research, execution, and sharing

Beyond capture and sync, AI Memo Wrap supports memo tags, content sharing, and structured output workflows.

1) One-Click Highlight Capture

Select text on any webpage and save with source URL, title, and timestamp.

2) AI Memo Wrapping

Convert snippets into concise, context-aware memos and action-ready tasks.

3) Memo Tags & Filters

Add tags, filter by #tag, and recover quickly using active filter chips.

4) Share Outputs

Copy/share single memo or digest output to teammates, docs, or social channels.

5) Notion Database Sync

Sync memos and tasks into your Notion database with stable structure.

6) Local-first + Optional Cloud

Use local storage by default and enable cloud sync when cross-device collaboration is needed.

7) Workflow Continuity Buffer

When an in-app AI panel resets during editing (for example in Shopify Flow email setup), keep key AI snippets in side notes and paste them back section by section.
