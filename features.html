Product capabilities

Features built for real research and daily execution

AI Memo Wrap is designed for creators, researchers, founders, and operators who want faster capture-to-action workflows.

1) One-Click Highlight Capture

Select text on any webpage and save it instantly with source URL, page title, and timestamp. No copy-paste loops.

2) AI Memo Wrapping

Transform raw snippets into concise, context-aware memos. You can keep original text plus AI summary in the same note block.

3) Task Extraction

Turn ideas into action items. AI can extract TODOs and decision points from your captured content for execution-oriented workflows.

4) Notion Database Sync

Send memos and tasks directly to your selected Notion database with pre-mapped fields to maintain structure at scale.

5) Source Traceability

Every memo keeps source metadata so you can jump back to original context anytime and avoid information drift.

6) Lightweight Browser Workflow

Works where your research happens: in-browser. No need to break focus by switching across multiple tools while reading.

Typical workflow

  1. Read an article, report, or document.
  2. Highlight key paragraph and click wrap.
  3. Generate AI summary and optional tasks.
  4. Sync the output to Notion as structured records.
  5. Review and execute from your knowledge base.
