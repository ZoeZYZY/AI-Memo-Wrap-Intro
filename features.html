Product capabilities

Features built for real research and daily execution

AI Memo Wrap is designed for creators, researchers, founders, and operators who want faster capture-to-action workflows.

1) One-Click Highlight Capture

Select text on any webpage and save it instantly with source URL, page title, and timestamp.

2) AI Memo Wrapping

Transform raw snippets into concise, context-aware memos while preserving the original quote.

3) Task Extraction

Extract TODOs and decision points from captured text for execution-oriented workflows.

4) Notion Database Sync

Send memos and tasks into your chosen Notion database with consistent structure.

5) Source Traceability

Keep source URL and context so every memo remains verifiable and reviewable.

6) Lightweight Browser Workflow

Capture and organize without leaving your browser reading flow.
